# Ithaca Energy / Summit E&P | Buy-Side Valuation

**Status:** Work in progress  
**Currency:** USD millions (USDm), unless otherwise stated

## Investment question

What is the maximum price Ithaca Energy should have been willing to pay for 100% of Summit E&P’s shares, and which assumptions would justify a lower bid?

## Transaction background

In 2022, Ithaca Energy acquired Summit Exploration and Production from Sumitomo Corporation. Summit held an interest in the Elgin-Franklin oil and gas fields in the UK North Sea, where Ithaca already owned a stake. The transaction closed on 30 June 2022 with a reported reference enterprise value of $148 million.

## Valuation approach

I will use Summit’s published accounts to build an independent valuation from Ithaca’s perspective.

1. Reconstruct Summit’s historical financial performance and identify its main revenue drivers.
2. Forecast cash flows from Summit’s share of production, including operating costs, capital expenditure and decommissioning costs.
3. Discount those cash flows to estimate enterprise value, then reconcile enterprise value to equity value to determine a maximum bid for the shares.
4. Cross-check the valuation against listed peers and test how commodity prices, production volumes and field closure timing affect the bid.
5. Compare the independently estimated enterprise value with the reported $148 million transaction benchmark.

## Planned deliverables

- **Excel valuation model** with historical financials, operating assumptions, valuation and sensitivity analysis.
- **Six-slide investment presentation (PDF)** setting out the recommended maximum bid, its rationale and the conditions that would lead to a lower offer.
- **Source register** linking financial inputs and transaction details to the underlying public documents.
